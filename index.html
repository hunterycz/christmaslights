About us

Holiday lighting that looks polished from the curb and effortless from your couch.

For over three years, Evergreen Lighting Co.'s professional holiday decorators have been illuminating the festive season for delighted residential and commercial customers.

Our clients come to us for a seamless, stress-free experience and stay for the clean rooflines, warm glow, reliable service, and careful attention to every detail.
